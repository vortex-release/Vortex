The weapon silhouettes are Valve CS2 UI assets extracted from the installed
game for this local build. Original asset ownership remains with Valve.
The project's MIT license does not relicense these game assets.
assets/weapons/manifest.json records their source paths and SHA-256 hashes.

The SAS preview model and base-color textures are also extracted from the
installed CS2 game. Original ownership remains with Valve.
assets/preview-model.json records their source, skeleton and exporter.
Exported using Source 2 Viewer 20.0 (ValveResourceFormat): https://s2v.app
