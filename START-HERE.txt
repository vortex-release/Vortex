VORTEX 3.16

Open Vortex.exe or use the Vortex desktop shortcut.
Launch: open Counter-Strike 2, select its process, then click Launch overlay.
Press Insert in the game to open the menu. Home toggles the overlay.
Open preview runs the simulated scene.

Overview: Signature, Focus, and Broadcast presets, palette cleanup, and Undo.
Players: labels, health, weapons, model effects, colors, and awareness.
Aim & recoil: tracking, weapon compensation, input calibration, and camera FOV.
Trajectories: bullet tracers, throw previews, and flight trails.
World: contrast, utility areas, particles, and motion.
Feedback: hitmarkers, damage, sounds, and objective information.
Settings: appearance, interface, and runtime diagnostics.
Save stores your choices. Enable Auto-save to keep changes automatically.

Updates appear when the publisher has configured a public update service.
Close the game and preview before selecting Install and restart.
If the update server is offline, the installed app continues to work.
This local preview installer has no public update URL yet.

Profiles: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vortex\OverlaySettings.ini
Logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vortex\logs
Older Desktop\CS2-Observer-Overlay profiles are imported once.
